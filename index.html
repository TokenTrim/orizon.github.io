The problem

    

      Production traces are how teams build evals. But when your data is sensitive, no one can read
      them, so teams fly blind or pay people to hand-write fake traces that
      look nothing like production.
    

  


  
  

    
What Orizon is

    

      

        
Orizon is your eval set at the scale of production.

        
Built from all of your real traffic, not a hand-picked subset.

      

      

        
Orizon is safe by construction.

        
No one ever reads a real user's trace. What you evaluate on is synthetic from the start.

      

      

        
Orizon is a component in your own stack.

        
It runs in front of your agent and writes synthetic traces to Braintrust, Langfuse,
          LangSmith, or Raindrop.

      

      

        
Orizon is not a new sub-processor.

        
No customer data ever reaches our servers, so there's nothing new for security to sign off on.
