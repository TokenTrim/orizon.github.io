Swap models, not behavior

Change the model behind your agent, not what your customers feel.

Orizon makes the model swappable underneath your production agent. You buy the behavior at a fixed price — which model delivers it is our problem.

Depend on one provider and you are at their mercy — when they raise prices, quietly change the model, or retire the one you built your business on.

The problem

Cheaper models are often good enough at a tenth of the price, and the cost-to-performance frontier moves every few months. Yet almost nobody switches. The prompt, guardrails and tool descriptions were all tuned to the original model, so swapping it quietly degrades the experience your customers rely on.

Only about one in ten teams that try to move providers actually manage it — so most keep overpaying by multiples on inference that is already a large slice of revenue. As agents do more of the work, that gap between the model you run and the model you could run only grows.

How it works

  1. Point your agent at us. We sit in front as a pass-through gateway, forwarding to your current model provider. Nothing changes for your customers on day one.
  2. We learn your agent from real traffic — the behavior your customers actually depend on, not a generic benchmark.
  3. We tune the context — prompts, tool descriptions, guardrails — for cheaper or newer candidate models until they match, and prove the match by shadowing your live production traffic.
  4. You approve the switch. We roll it out slowly with monitoring, rollback and accountability, and keep finding you better, cheaper models over time.

Why it pays

Evaluating a new model is a whole project for your team, and it should not be their focus. For us it is one automated system, amortized across every customer — plus wholesale pricing you cannot get on your own. When a switch is a genuine capability jump, the savings are not a slim margin; the same quality can cost an order of magnitude less than the frontier model it replaces.
